# Case studies and benchmarks

*Applied evidence: documented cases and benchmarks, with their methods and limits.*

## State of the art

**Benchmarks**
- As of June 2026, a vendor-authored preprint (Ranqo) on 100,000+ responses and 100+ brands found global brands appear in 73% of relevant answers, mid-market brands in 44% and niche brands in 11%; about 78% of citations go to corporate sites; ranked listicles are the top format (about 21% of citations); sentiment changes about 6.7 times more often than presence. [arXiv 2606.20065](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20065) [single source: vendor-linked, not peer reviewed]
- As of May 2026, GEO-Bench compares ranking-manipulation methods under one protocol on a small open model; relevant as a benchmark, not as evidence about commercial engines. [GEO-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29107) [single source: preprint]
- As of July 2026, a critical survey finds no reviewed technique shows a stable cross-platform effect on organic discoverability or downstream behaviour. [Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14035) [single source: preprint]

**Applied evidence**
- B2B, as of November 2026: in a survey of 200 software-buyer experiences (with Wynter, in which the author has invested), AI chats added a new vendor for 62.7% of 158 respondents who already had vendors in mind; 72.5% visited vendor sites and 48% searched Google afterwards; 78 of 126 who chose a vendor said it appeared in the first answer. Self-reported. [Growth Memo](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/how-software-buyers-use-ai) [single source]
- As of 2026, a study of 100 B2B "best" queries found brands' own listicles were cited but omitted from the recommendation about 69% of the time; see [How answer engines work](how-answer-engines-work.html). [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-overviews-cite-self-serving-listicles-recommend-competitors-480573)
- Agency and vendor case studies reporting multi-fold AI visibility or trial gains were found by open search but not ingested: baselines, periods and attribution are self-reported and no independent confirmation was found (see the run report).

## Open contradictions

- **Do vendor-reported gains translate into traffic?** Agency case studies claim large lifts, while independent studies cited by SparkToro find AI referrals a small share of traffic ([SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/is-ai-visibility-a-marketing-boondoggle)); Fishkin also notes that success cases often lack a baseline. The case studies themselves are not ingested.
