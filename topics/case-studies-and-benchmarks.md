# Case studies and benchmarks

*Applied evidence: documented cases and benchmarks, with their methods and limits.*

## State of the art

**Benchmarks and cases**
- GEO-bench, introduced with the GEO paper (November 2023), is the reference benchmark for testing optimization methods ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735)). [single source]
- As of October 2026, one single-site opt-out test is documented; full detail in [Measurement and impact](measurement-and-impact.html) ([Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-ctr-42421.html)). [single source]
- As of August 2026, the largest experimental evidence on click loss is the 1,100-participant field experiment, detailed in [Measurement and impact](measurement-and-impact.html) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18352)). [single source]
- Vendor case studies (for example results quoted in Sitecore's June 2026 Scrunch release) were not added here: they are company-stated and without a method.
