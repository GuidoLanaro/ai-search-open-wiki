# Measurement and impact

*How visibility and traffic from answer engines are measured, and what the data says about click loss and user behaviour.*

## State of the art

**First-party measurement**
- As of June 2026, Search Console has Search Generative AI performance reports showing impressions of a site's URLs in AI Overviews, AI Mode and Discover, by page, country and date (device for Search only); impressions are the only metric, and the reports reached all sites worldwide as of 31 August 2026. Clicks stay inside the overall Web report. [Google Search Central Blog](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports), [documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) [single source: official]
- As of November 2026, OpenAI offers a Business Hub (beta from 10 November) with mentions, clicks, CTR and top topics over 7 or 28 days. According to Peec AI, it does not show prompts, fan-out queries, competitors or recommendations, and CTR was below 1% for nearly every brand it checked. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt), [Peec AI](https://peec.ai/blog/chatgpt-business-hub) [single source: vendor review]

**Click loss and traffic**
- As of November 2026, a meta-analysis of 32 studies finds clicks fall when AI answers appear but the size varies by design: Pew 8% vs 15% of visits with a click; Ahrefs position-one CTR 1.6% vs a modeled 3.7%; a field experiment -39.8% outbound organic clicks; Wikipedia referrals -5.45%; Semrush/Datos found a slight zero-click decline. No pooled estimate exists. [Growth Memo](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/ai-search-meta-analysis-q4-2026) [corroborated: several independent studies, differing results]
- As of November 2026, Similarweb panel data show browser visits to the web 12.2% below the Q1 2022 peak and visits per user down about 29%, without attributing this to AI. [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-traffic-to-the-open-web-has-fallen-24-in-5-years) [single source]
- As of October 2026, practitioner studies cited by Rand Fishkin find AI referral traffic small: all LLMs 0.73% of traffic across 100 sites, organic about 39 times AI traffic across 95 UK sites. [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/is-ai-visibility-a-marketing-boondoggle) [corroborated: two independent studies, both secondary]

**User behaviour**
- As of November 2026, in a 12,321-user US ChatGPT panel, 12.1% of responses included a brand and 6.9% of prompts involved researching a brand to buy from; the author argues Google remains the main driver of AI visibility. [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/we-analyzed-11800-chatgpt-prompts-from-12000-users-to-answer-how-do-americans-use-ai-tools-in-2026) [single source]
- As of November 2026, Semrush reports AI Overviews on 88% of 400 tracked US commercial keywords (banking, ecommerce, software), up from about 46% a year earlier. [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews-commercial-modifiers-study) [single source: vendor, narrow sample]
- As of September 2026, Profound reports that a page's median citation share halves 11 days after its peak (883,000 pages, seven engines), so snapshot metrics age quickly. [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/the-half-life-of-an-ai-citation-is-11-days) [single source: vendor]
- B2B: as of November 2026, a survey of 200 software buyers found AI chats added a vendor to the shortlist for 62.7% of those with vendors in mind. See [Case studies and benchmarks](case-studies-and-benchmarks.html).

## Open contradictions

- **How much do AI answers cost in clicks?** Estimates range from about 5% (Wikipedia referrals) to 40-58% fewer clicks, while Semrush/Datos found only a slight zero-click drop for keywords gaining AI Overviews ([Growth Memo](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/ai-search-meta-analysis-q4-2026)). The studies measure different things (referrals, CTR, zero-click share) and are not directly comparable.
- **Is AI visibility worth budget?** Rand Fishkin argues it should be funded as a brand channel and not a traffic channel, given tiny referral shares ([SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/is-ai-visibility-a-marketing-boondoggle)); vendors report visibility effects on brand-site visits (Profound: +2.07 percentage points in seven days after a ChatGPT mention, cited in [Growth Memo](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/ai-search-meta-analysis-q4-2026)). A Growth Memo test of 12 sites found no reliable link between AI visibility and branded or direct traffic.
