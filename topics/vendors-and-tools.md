# Vendors and tools

*The landscape of AI visibility tracking and optimization tools: features, methods, pricing, funding and acquisitions.*

## State of the art

**Consolidation and funding**
- As of June 2026, Sitecore announced the acquisition of Scrunch, an AI visibility vendor; the price was not disclosed, and Bloomberg reportedly put it near 225 million dollars ([Sitecore](https://www.sitecore.com/de-de/company/newsroom/press-releases/2026/06/sitecore-acquires-scrunch-to-help-brands-influence-discovery--and-buying-decisions)). [single source] Results quoted in the release (for example Akamai's +364% appearances in AI answers) are Scrunch's own figures.
- As of September 2026, Profound announced a 180M dollar Series D at a 1.8B dollar valuation, less than seven months after a 96M dollar Series C, and says it serves over 1,000 enterprise brands; it is adding an agent orchestrator and Ads Studio for ads in AI search ([Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/newsroom/profound-raises-usd180m-series-d-at-usd1-8b-valuation-to-build-the-ai-platform-for-marketing-teams)). [single source] All figures are company-stated.
- Pattern: tracking vendors are being absorbed into content and experience suites (Sitecore) or are widening into execution and advertising (Profound). Search-based reports of the Adobe–Semrush deal (completed April 2026) were not ingested for lack of a primary source.

**Measurement caveat**
- Vendors' fan-out and citation studies (for example Peec AI, [April 2026](https://docs.peec.ai/research/fanouts-per-prompt-by-engine), and Ahrefs) are vendor data and can differ in method; see [How answer engines work](how-answer-engines-work.html).
