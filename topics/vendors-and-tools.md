# Vendors and tools

*The landscape of AI visibility tracking and optimization tools: features, methods, pricing, funding and acquisitions.*

## State of the art

**Funding and consolidation**
- As of September 2026, Profound's own company announcement states a Series D of 180 million USD at a 1.8 billion USD valuation led by Sequoia Capital and Kleiner Perkins. [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/series-d) [single source]
- Other 2026 deals and rounds surfaced by open search were not ingested this run because they fall before the window or lack a primary source; see the run report.

**First-party tools are arriving**
- As of 2026, Google (Search Console generative AI reports) and OpenAI (Business Hub, beta since 10 November) now supply first-party AI visibility data, though limited to impressions or mentions, clicks and topics. Vendors position their products on what these omit, such as prompts, competitors and recommendation, according to a vendor review (Peec AI). Detail on [Measurement and impact](measurement-and-impact.html). [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt), [Peec AI](https://peec.ai/blog/chatgpt-business-hub) [single source]

**Method caveat**
- Vendor research often sells the metric it measures (for example citation decay, tracked by Profound); weigh it accordingly. [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/the-half-life-of-an-ai-citation-is-11-days)
