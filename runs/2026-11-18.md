# Run 2026-11-18 (run zero)

Window: 2026-05-18 to 2026-11-18, plus foundational pieces. 22 items added, 5 topic pages written, 5 vendor rows updated.

## (a) Highlights
- **Run zero.** The five topic pages were written from empty templates; the wiki now holds 22 items.
- **Google settled its position.** The May 2026 guide says AI features are rooted in core Search, AEO and GEO are not separate disciplines, and llms.txt, chunking and special schema are not used or needed. Search Console gained Generative AI performance reports (impressions only, global by 31 Aug 2026), and Google backs a shared robots.txt AI training token (draft IETF work, support planned 2027).
- **First-party measurement from OpenAI.** Businesses in ChatGPT (beta, 10 Nov 2026) adds a Business Hub with mentions, clicks and CTR; according to Peec AI it omits prompts, competitors and recommendations.
- **The click-loss evidence is mixed.** A Q4 2026 meta-analysis of 32 studies finds clicks fall with AI answers but by anywhere from about 5% to 58%. SparkToro-reported data put AI referrals at under 1% of traffic, and the ChatGPT panel data show 6.9% of prompts involve brand purchase research.
- **GEO evidence is narrower than the hype.** A July 2026 survey finds the original GEO gains hold only for already-retrieved content. Profound reports a median citation half-life of 11 days (vendor data), and Profound raised a 180M USD Series D at a 1.8B USD valuation.
