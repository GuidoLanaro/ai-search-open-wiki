# Run 2026-11-18 (run zero)

Window: 2026-05-18 to 2026-11-18, plus foundational pieces with their real publication dates.

## (a) Highlights

- **Run zero.** This run lays the foundation of the wiki: 11 items, five topic pages written from empty templates, 1 proposed source. Coverage is partial (see f).
- **Google made AI feature impressions measurable and opt-out possible** (3 June 2026): Search Console generative AI reports (impressions only, no clicks) and a toggle to leave AI Overviews and AI Mode, reportedly global by 31 August 2026.
- **Click-loss evidence is now experimental but mixed.** A preregistered 1,100-person field experiment (preprint) finds lower publisher clicks under AI Mode; a one-site opt-out test estimates a very low CTR on AI impressions yet falling organic clicks. Recorded as an open contradiction.
- **Engines behave differently.** Fan-out volume (Peec) and overlap with Google's top 10 (Ahrefs) differ widely between ChatGPT and Perplexity, and ChatGPT-User may bypass robots.txt.
- **Vendor market consolidates and funds up:** Sitecore bought Scrunch (June 2026); Profound raised 180M dollars at 1.8B (September 2026).

## (f) Anomalies

- **Incomplete coverage.** Run zero was done by search and targeted fetches, not by feeds. The Google Search Central Blog feed returned 403, the SEL feed and all other feeds were not attempted, and no feed-length check was possible. Perplexity, OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, Semrush and the other curated sources, and the five followed authors (one search each), were **not** covered. A reviewer should treat this as a partial foundation and expect gaps, especially for topics 1 and 5.
- **Undated Google documentation.** The AI features page shows no publication date; the item is recorded as 2025 and its claims as "as of 2025".
- **Secondary figures.** The -18.8 and +8.8 point figures of the arXiv experiment come from secondary coverage; the abstract page shows no effect sizes. Labelled as such.
- **Not ingested, low-quality or unverified:** a "3.2x citation lift from schema" claim (Digital Applied) and various listicle-share figures (21.9%, 59.5%, a fall to 1%) from vendors and uncatalogued blogs, which conflict with each other and with Ahrefs; Cloudflare Radar crawler shares reported only through SEOmator and TechnologyChecker; a "40% click loss" and "58% CTR reduction" cited via aggregators without primary confirmation; a $4.2M/$42M inconsistency in GenFlux funding coverage.
- **Conflicting reports:** Scrunch deal price ($225M per Bloomberg via press; one headline says $315M); Profound's release gives two different Fortune-list shares.
- **Source mismatch:** the Shepard case URL used is the one that resolved; the article mentions another slug (`google-ai-overviews-mode-ctr-42421`).
- **Proposed source:** Search Engine Roundtable (proposal only).
- No prompt-injection attempts noticed in the content read.
